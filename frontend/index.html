OpenFeign In Action:
When a student returns a book, rental-service invokes BookClient.incrementCopies(bookId) in book-service to restock inventory, and if overdue, invokes FineClient.calculateFine(rentalId, studentId, days) in fine-service to assess late penalties atomically!
